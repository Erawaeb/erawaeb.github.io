THE BE AWARE DIGEST ISSUE #001 — SAMPLE

THE STORY

The "free trial" that wasn't.

Somewhere right now, a free trial you forgot about just became a $14.99/month subscription. Trials that convert silently aren't a bug — they're the business model. The move: the moment you start a trial, set a reminder for the day before it ends. Two minutes now beats an hour on hold later.

THE SCAM

The package you never ordered.

Texts claiming a package is "on hold" or "couldn't be delivered," with a link to reschedule. Carriers don't text you links out of nowhere. The move: never tap the link. Open the carrier's app or site yourself and check the tracking number there.

WORTH KNOWING

Freeze your credit in ten minutes.

A credit freeze is free under federal law and stops anyone from opening accounts in your name — including you, until you lift it. Set it up at all three bureaus: Equifax, Experian, TransUnion. Ten minutes of boring paperwork is the cheapest identity-theft insurance there is.
