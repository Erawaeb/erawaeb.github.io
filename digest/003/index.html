THE BE AWARE DIGEST ISSUE #003 — SEP 16, 2026

THE STORY

The checkout that shopped for you.

Pre-checked boxes, "sneak-into-basket" extras, and cancel buttons hidden three menus deep. Dark patterns aren't accidents — they're A/B tested. The move: slow down at checkout, uncheck everything you didn't ask for, and screenshot the final total.

THE SCAM

The job that pays you to do tasks.

A "recruiter" finds you, the work is easy — like videos, rate products — and the pay looks great. Then you need to "unlock" your earnings with a deposit. Real jobs pay you. They never charge you to get paid.

WORTH KNOWING

Your phone number is the key to your bank.

SIM-swapping lets a thief move your number to their phone and intercept your login codes. The move: put a PIN on your carrier account and switch your important logins to an authenticator app instead of text-message codes.
