THE BE AWARE DIGEST DAILY RED FLAG #016 — SEP 28, 2026

THE SCAM

The tipster with the guaranteed win.

His record looks unbeatable: win after win after win, all over his Telegram, Discord, or Instagram. For a fee — sometimes small, sometimes a cut of your winnings — this tipster will send you the "lock" for tonight's game. It looks like easy money because his whole feed is winning slips.

Here's the trick, and it's an old one. He sends half his followers one team and the other half the other team. One half always wins. The losing half hears nothing more; the winning half gets a message asking for payment. Nobody can predict games — but nobody has to, when you split the audience and only talk to the winners.

THE RED FLAG

The word "guaranteed" sitting next to a bet. A bet can't be guaranteed — that's what the odds are for. Everything else around it is stagecraft: win slips with no timestamps, losing posts that quietly get deleted, and a track record that only exists on his own page.

Run the business logic. If he could actually beat the odds, selling tips for $49 would be the worst business model on earth. Real edges get bet quietly; only fake ones get advertised.

THE RULE

Never pay for picks. Nobody sells a real edge for the price of a pizza — the picks are the product, and you're the buyer, which means you were always the one paying.
