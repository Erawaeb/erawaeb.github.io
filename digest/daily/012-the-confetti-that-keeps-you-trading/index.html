THE BE AWARE DIGEST DAILY RED FLAG #012 — SEP 24, 2026

THE SCAM

The confetti that keeps you trading.

It looks like investing. It plays like a casino. You sign up for a trading app and get a free stock — but only after you start trading. Every buy or sell triggers a little celebration: Robinhood's famous confetti burst fired every time a trade went through. Push notifications nudge you back in all day — "your stock is moving!" — and fractional shares mean every tap costs almost nothing, so tapping feels free.

The game isn't beating the market. The game is keeping you tapping, because the app gets paid for your orders whether you win or lose. Robinhood's main income has long been payment for order flow — money that market makers pay it for the right to handle your trades. More taps, more money for the house. In this game, you are not the player. You are the product.

THE RED FLAG

Wins feel huge and losses feel quiet. Gains flash green with celebration; losses sit there in small gray numbers. The notifications arrive like slot-machine nudges — just often enough to pull you back in, never enough to feel like pressure. And the free-stock signup hook makes starting feel like winning.

Ask one question: if this product wanted you to invest carefully, would it look like a game? Spreadsheets don't throw confetti. Casinos do.

THE RULE

Treat confetti as a warning label. Turn off every push notification from the app, move it off your home screen (or delete it and use the website instead), and decide your trades in advance — write down the price you'd buy or sell at, then walk away. If trading feels exciting, that's not your instincts talking. That's the design.

And if you want to see how these products are engineered to keep you playing, read the House Edge guide at https://erawaeb.com/guides/the-house-edge/.

(Source: Robinhood removed its trade confetti in 2021 after Massachusetts regulators flagged the gamification; the SEC fined the company $65 million in 2020 over payment-for-order-flow disclosures — widely reported.)
