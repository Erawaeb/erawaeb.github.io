THE BE AWARE DIGEST DAILY RED FLAG #003 — SEP 16, 2026

THE SCAM

The 'wrong number' text that empties your wallet.

"Hey, is this still your number? It's been ages!" A wrong-number text. You reply politely — and instead of apologizing and moving on, they keep chatting. Friendly. Funny. Patient. Weeks pass. Maybe months.

Then investing comes up. They "trade crypto" on a special platform and "never lose." You start small, and the dashboard shows your money doubling. You even withdraw a little — it works. So you add more. A lot more.

Then one day the platform locks. "Withdrawals" suddenly need "tax fees" you keep paying, hoping to unlock your money. And your new friend is gone — along with everything. This is called "pig butchering": they fatten you up before the slaughter. It's cost victims billions, and the person texting you may be a trafficking victim forced to run the scam themselves.

THE RED FLAG

Real investing platforms don't arrive through a wrong-number text. The dashboard gains are fake numbers on a website the scammers built; the small early "withdrawals" are bait, paid from your own deposit to build trust.

The red flag is the sequence itself: a stranger who won't stop chatting, a sudden turn to investing, and any fee you must pay to unlock your own money. Real advisors don't cold-text strangers, and no legitimate platform holds your funds hostage for a "tax fee."

THE RULE

Never invest through a platform a stranger handed you — check whether it's registered with regulators yourself, on a site you typed in by hand.

If a "wrong number" keeps chatting, block it. And never pay a fee to withdraw your own money: that's the moment the trap closes, and paying it only deepens the loss.

(Source: FBI IC3 2024 Internet Crime Report; reporting on pig-butchering / crypto investment fraud.)
