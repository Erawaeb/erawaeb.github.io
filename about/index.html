THE NAMEERAWAEB.

It's "be aware" spelled backwards — a word that only makes sense when you look at it twice. That's the whole brand in seven letters. Most of life's expensive mistakes come from looking once: the text you tapped without reading, the trial you started without checking the terms, the headline you shared without verifying. ERAWAEB is the reminder to look twice.

THE MISSIONAttention literacy.

The modern world is engineered to harvest your attention and monetize your inattention. Dark patterns, phishing, fine print, viral misinformation — these aren't random hazards. They're industries, with budgets and A/B tests and quarterly targets, and you are the product being optimized.

Being aware isn't paranoia. It's literacy — a learnable skill, like reading. ERAWAEB exists to teach it: one nightly email, one short video, one field guide at a time. Short enough to finish. Sharp enough to matter.

WHAT IT ISThree things.

  • The Digest — one short email every night. One story, one scam, one thing worth knowing.
  • The Channel — faceless short-form video. Scam breakdowns, fact checks, fine-print explainers, know-your-rights clips.
  • The Guides — free field guides for high-stakes moments: scam defense, traffic stops, trial traps.

WHAT IT ISN'TClear boundaries.

  • Not a news outlet. We don't chase breaking news or outrage. We cover what helps you, not what hooks you.
  • Not financial or legal advice. We explain how things work in plain language. For real money or legal problems, talk to a licensed professional.
  • Not affiliated with anyone. ERAWAEB is an independent project. No sponsors, no parent company, no agenda beyond sharper readers.
  • Not fear. Awareness without anxiety. If something here ever makes you feel paranoid instead of prepared, we're doing it wrong — tell us.

The deal: we respect your attention like it's money — because it is. Three minutes a night. No noise. Just the signal.
